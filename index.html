A short bio.

I am a learning scientist and child development specialist. My obsession is turning cognitive-science research into things parents and educators can actually use on a Tuesday night.

I spent 8+ years designing curricula and training teachers across India and the US — at Teach for India, the Shiv Nadar Foundation, LEAD EdTech — then went deep on the science with an MS in Education and Educational Psychology at Northwestern University.

My work sits at the intersection of early childhood development, EdTech, and AI in education. I read the research so you don't have to.
