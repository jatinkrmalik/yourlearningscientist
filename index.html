About

I'm a learning scientist with an MS in Education and Social Policy (Learning Sciences and Educational Technology) from Northwestern. On Your Learning Scientist I turn child-development research into short, practical videos for parents and educators.

For 8+ years I designed curricula and trained teachers across India and the US, at Teach for India, the Shiv Nadar Foundation, and LEAD EdTech. At LEAD I analyzed learning data for 1.2M+ students across thousands of schools. Now I share what the research actually says about screens, books, language, bias, and how kids learn.
